feat(builtins): add object.remove/filter, array.slice, trim helpers and range_step

Six builtins that policy authors reach for constantly and that the initial
cut omitted:

- object.remove / object.filter — drop or keep selected keys
- array.slice — with OPA semantics for non-positive indices
- trim_prefix / trim_suffix
- numbers.range_step — with an explicit step and a clear error for step 0

Builtin count goes from 46 to 52.
